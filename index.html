Bearly Chat demo

A practice walkthrough. Nothing you do here is saved or requests your location.

Having trouble installing? Open https://tmpark12.github.io/Bearly-Chat/ in a browser, then select Install app.

1. Pick a feeling

😊 Happy😢 Sad😠 Mad

Ask the child to choose a face that matches how they feel. In the popup, choose Yes to confirm or No to choose another face. After confirming, choose whether to add another feeling, up to three different feelings. Choose No to continue to colors.

Use Hear the question to play the question again when a device voice is available.

2. Pick a color

RedBlueYellow

Choose any color that feels right. In the popup, choose Yes to continue or No to choose another color.

Select Back to feelings to choose the feelings again.

3. Add notes or skip

The device owner may add optional notes, or choose Skip notes and continue. Choose Back to colors to change the color.

Notes are included in saved check-ins and CSV exports.

4. Allow location to save

An adult can choose Allow location and save and approve the browser's location request. Location is required; if permission is denied or location is unavailable, the check-in is not saved. Choose Cancel to return without saving.

The feelings, color, notes, date and time, location, and browser/device information are stored in this browser on this device.

5. Download or review

After saving, Save and Download Check-In downloads a CSV with the saved check-in, including location and device information. Its approximate location link contains the recorded coordinates; opening the link shares those coordinates with Google Maps. Close keeps the saved check-in on the device without downloading it.

Choose Saved check-ins to review or export records. After downloading, verify the file was saved before choosing whether to clear the downloaded records. Exports contain location and device information, so keep them private. Records do not sync between devices or browsers.
