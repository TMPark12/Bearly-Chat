Bearly Chat Privacy Policy

November 19, 2026 · @Tina

Summary

Bearly Chat keeps check-ins on your own device and does not send them to the developer. This policy took effect on November 19, 2026.

  • There is no account, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Saved check-ins stay in the browser on the device where they were made.
  • Saving and location are off by default. An adult can turn them on in Settings.
  • Exported files belong to whoever exports them, who is responsible for keeping them private.
  • The app is made for a young child to use with an adult beside them.

What the app saves

When saving is on, each check-in stores:

  • the feelings and color the child chose
  • any note an adult chose to type
  • the local date and time, the matching UTC timestamp, and the time zone offset
  • a description of the browser and device (the browser's own text, not a unique device ID)
  • location coordinates and their accuracy, only if an adult enabled location and allowed it in the browser

The app remembers settings such as voice, language, text size and whether saving is on. If an adult enables multiple users, the app also stores the user names they enter and associates new saved check-ins with the selected user. Choose non-identifying nicknames; user names are stored even when Do Not Save is on. Profiles are not protected by a password.

All of this is stored in the browser's own storage on the device. The app does not request age, photo, email address, contact list, advertising identifier or account. It does not use the microphone or camera, and the voice you hear is provided by the device or browser.

With Do Not Save turned on, a new check-in is not stored. Previously saved check-ins remain until cleared.

Location, notes and exports

Location. The app never asks for location unless an adult turns on "Save location with check-ins" in Settings and turns off Do Not Save. The browser asks permission first. New check-ins saved with location off have no coordinates, accuracy or map link; older check-ins can still contain previously saved location.

Notes. Notes are optional and meant for the adult who owns the device. Please do not put a child's full name, address, school or case details in a note.

Exports. An adult can export saved check-ins as a CSV file. The file can include notes and, if location was saved, coordinates and a Google Maps link. Opening that link shares those coordinates with Google. Export files are not encrypted, so keep them private and delete copies you no longer need.

Hosting and third parties

The app is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub can see ordinary web request information, such as an IP address, when the app is loaded, and handles it under its own privacy statement. The developer does not receive check-ins and does not run analytics.

The app does not load fonts, scripts or advertising from other websites.

The voice that reads feelings aloud is provided by the device or browser. Depending on the device, some voices may use an online speech service run by the device maker, under that company's policies; spoken text may be sent to that service.

The developer does not sell or share information, because the developer does not receive it.

Children's privacy

Bearly Chat is made for young children to use with a parent, foster parent or other trusted adult. An adult should set it up, decide whether saving or location is on, and handle any exports.

The developer does not collect, receive or have access to any child's information through the app. Check-ins stay on the device, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics. A parent or guardian can delete stored data using the steps under Retention and deletion.

If you believe a child's information has reached the developer, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Foster care and agency use

Children in foster care are protected by confidentiality rules that are stricter than this policy. Families and agencies should follow those rules and their agency's instructions first.

  • Use Bearly Chat in a foster or agency setting only with the agency's permission.
  • Use non-identifying nicknames for profiles. Do not enter names, addresses, court or case details, or anything that identifies a child or birth family.
  • Treat a check-in as an observation of a mood at one moment. It is not a case record, an assessment, or evidence about any event or person.
  • Share exported files only as the agency's rules allow.

Retention and deletion

Saved check-ins stay on the device until cleared. The developer cannot delete them for you, because the developer never has them. To remove them:

  1. Open Saved check-ins, export the records if you want a copy, then choose File saved - clear downloaded data. In multi-user mode, this applies to the selected user.
  2. To clear all saved check-ins, including copies in the old app database, choose Clear saved check-ins in Settings. To remove settings and user names as well, clear this site's data in the browser's settings.
  3. Delete any exported CSV files and printed copies yourself.

Security. The app loads its own files and does not send check-ins to the developer. Browser storage is not encrypted by the app, so anyone who can use an unlocked device can see what is saved. Use a screen lock and device encryption, and keep exports private.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with a new date at the top. If a change affects what the app collects, it will be described in the app as well.
