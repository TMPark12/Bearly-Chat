Using Bearly Chat

Bearly Chat is a free feelings check-in for young children to use with a parent, foster parent or other trusted adult. By using it, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

An adult must supervise a child's use and is responsible for the settings, the device, and any saved or exported check-ins. The app is provided as is, and features may change or stop at any time.

Not medical, therapy or legal advice

Bearly Chat is a communication aid. It does not diagnose, treat or assess anyone, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, therapist, counselor, caseworker or lawyer.

A check-in shows what a child tapped at one moment. It does not prove how a child feels, and it is not a finding or evidence about any event or person. Adults should use it to start a conversation, and should not rely on it to make decisions about a child's care, safety or custody.

If a child may be in danger, or you are worried about a child's health or safety, contact local emergency services, the child's doctor or the child's caseworker right away.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep the device locked and protected, and keep saved check-ins and exported files private. See the Privacy Policy for how to clear them.
  • Do not enter names, addresses, case details or other identifying information in notes.
  • In a foster or agency setting, follow the agency's confidentiality rules and use the app only with its permission.
  • Do not use the app to harass, track or harm anyone, or to try to break or misuse it.
