# Bearly Chat

A simple check-in for toddlers to pick up to three feelings and a color that matches how they feel.

Feeling choices appear as colorful segments of a friendly caterpillar, with a layout that adapts to smaller screens.

Serve the app over HTTPS or localhost to use optional location, installation and offline support. To install it on a device, publish all app files (including the `icons` folder) together on an HTTPS web host. No account is required.

Open **Settings** to shuffle the feeling and color choices with **Randomize**, select a device-provided male or female voice (female by default), change speech volume, or mute speech. The interface defaults to English and also supports Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. **Do Not Save** is on by default: new check-ins and notes are not stored, location is not requested, and save/download options are disabled. Turn it off in Settings to save check-ins; an existing explicit preference is preserved. Previously saved check-ins remain until cleared. **Save location with check-ins** is off by default; enabling it requests location when saving. The **Help** link explains the app and installation; the privacy notice describes data handling.

**Save for multiple users** is off by default. When enabled, choose a user in Settings or add one using the name dialog. Names are saved in this browser even when **Do Not Save** is on; use non-identifying nicknames. Profiles are not accounts and have no password protection. Each saved check-in belongs to the selected user, and history and exports show only that user's records. Existing unassigned check-ins remain separate and can be viewed by turning multi-user mode off. Profiles stay in browser storage when check-ins are cleared; clear the site's browser data to remove names and settings. Changing users does not turn saving on. In supported browsers, exporting a user's history creates a separate named folder inside `checkins`; otherwise it downloads a user-named CSV. The single-check-in download remains a separate browser download.

The privacy policy and terms of use are linked from Settings and are available offline after the app is cached.

After choosing a feeling, a popup shows and speaks its name when a device voice is available. Choose **Yes** to confirm or **No** to choose another feeling. After confirming, choose whether to add another feeling, up to three different feelings; choose **No** to continue to colors. You can also continue from the feelings screen after choosing to add another. Returning from colors lets you choose the feelings again; the previous selections remain until a new feeling is confirmed. Choosing a color likewise opens a popup that shows and speaks its name; choose **Yes** to continue or **No** to choose another color. When **Do Not Save** is on, the notes page is skipped and the check-in can be completed without saving. When it is off, the device owner can optionally add notes and an adult can save the check-in. On the notes page, press Enter to continue to the save confirmation or Shift+Enter to add a new line. If location is enabled in Settings, location permission and a location fix are required to save; otherwise no location is requested or stored. Each saved check-in contains the feelings, color, notes, local date and time, UTC timestamp, UTC-minus-local offset in minutes, and the browser-provided device/browser description (not a unique device ID); if enabled, it also contains coordinates and reported location accuracy. Multiple feelings are stored together in the existing Mood field in exports. Older check-ins may contain location and may lack device description or notes. Records are stored in a local browser database and are not sent by the app to a server. Browser storage can be cleared or unavailable, and records do not sync between browsers or devices. Location may use GPS, Wi-Fi, or other device services; when enabled, offline saving requires the device to determine its location without a connection.

After saving, the final popup offers **Do another check-in** to start a fresh check-in, **Close** to close the chat, and **Save and Download Check-In** to download the complete current check-in as `check-in.csv`, including device details and, if saved, location and an approximate location link. The link contains the recorded coordinates, which may be precise even though the location is approximate; opening it shares those coordinates with Google Maps. No place name or city is looked up, and the link is blank if coordinates are unavailable. Creating the CSV works offline and does not require an API key. Check-ins waiting to be downloaded remain queued across app updates and are listed under **Saved check-ins**. After a download starts or a folder export completes, verify the file was saved: browser downloads can fail or be cancelled after starting. Choose **File saved - clear downloaded data** to remove only the exported records, **File saved - keep data** to keep them while marking them downloaded, or **Not sure - keep data** to leave them queued. Dismissing the popup also keeps the records. **Clear saved check-ins** in Settings removes all saved records across all users. Select **Saved check-ins** to review or export records. **Export checkins.csv** asks you to choose a parent folder in browsers that support folder access; it creates a `checkins` folder there and creates or replaces `checkins.csv` with the current user's records (or unassigned records when multi-user mode is off), including notes, any saved location, and device data. In other browsers, the file downloads to the browser's download location. Exports are snapshots and are not updated automatically. Exports may contain sensitive information: keep them private and delete exported copies separately. Browser or device storage can still be cleared or evicted outside the app's control.

The installed app offers a demo on its first launch, when the browser can detect that it is installed. You can also select **Help** at the top of the page and **Start demo** at any time for a step-by-step walkthrough that does not save a check-in or request location. Help and the demo link to the app's website for installation troubleshooting. Choose **Print or save as PDF** in Help or the demo to print all the walkthrough steps and the website link, or select **Save as PDF** in the device's print options, where available. The demo leaves an unfinished check-in unchanged and is available offline after the app has been cached. Clearing browser storage can cause the first-launch offer to appear again.

In **Settings**, choose **Settings demo** for a guided explanation of each settings area without changing any settings. Choose **Print or save Settings guide as PDF** there, or **Print or save as PDF** in the Settings demo, to print all its sections or save them as a PDF using the device's print options. The main demo and its printable guide explain the current saving, location, notes, and multi-user flow.

An install popup asks whether you want to add Bearly Chat to your home screen once per browser-tab session, or select **Install app** in the header at any time. Adding the app always requires your confirmation in the browser or device; the app cannot install itself. Installation does not request or depend on location; if enabled in Settings, location is requested later when saving a check-in. Supported browsers show their install prompt; otherwise the app shows instructions for your device. On iPhone and iPad, tap Share and choose **Add to Home Screen**; if that option is missing, use Safari. On a Mac, Safari offers **File > Add to Dock**. Chrome and Edge also offer installation from their menus or address bars. The browser and operating system control installation, and availability varies by device. Device location services may need to be enabled in settings; the app cannot turn them on automatically. Installed apps may have separate location permissions and storage from the browser, so existing check-ins might not appear in the installed app.

Open the app online once and allow the offline cache to finish installing before using it offline. The header shows the browser's reported online or offline status, which does not guarantee internet access. Offline check-ins require a device location fix only if location saving is enabled; device-provided speech voices may not be available offline. Google Maps links require a connection to open.

On the first saved-data operation after the storage rename, check-ins are copied to the Bearly Chat database. Close older app tabs before updating or clearing data; they can otherwise continue saving to the old database. Clearing all check-ins also clears the old backup. Clearing downloaded check-ins removes their matching backup copies while retaining other records. Downloaded files are separate and must be deleted manually.

The first screen offers **Hear voice** to preview or retry the device voice. The app prefers a female voice identified by its device-provided name and uses the same selected voice for questions, choices, and thank-you messages until previewed again. Names may not accurately indicate voice gender; if no matching voice is found, the app reports the fallback, which may sound different. Voices vary by device and may load later, require a download or internet connection, or be unavailable. Some browsers, including Safari on iOS, require a tap before speech can play: use **Hear the question** on the first screen. The color question is spoken after selecting feelings. The sound button can repeat either question.

When the installed app opens, it checks for updates before starting a check-in. If an update is ready, it installs and reloads automatically; saved check-ins remain on the device. If the check fails or takes too long, the app offers **Continue without updating**. A later update or an update in a browser tab shows an **Update app** notice instead, so an unfinished check-in is not interrupted. Existing installations running an older version may need to select **Update app** once to receive this startup behavior. Updates require a connection and service-worker support. Bump the cache version in `service-worker.js` whenever publishing app-file changes so the browser detects a new worker and refreshes the offline cache. The browser and operating system control installed app names and icons; changes may be delayed or require user approval even after the app updates. For icon changes, publish icons under new filenames and update the manifest paths so browsers can detect them.
